WHAT WE DO

Everything a tire shop does, minus the shop

01

New tires

Tell us your size and brand preference — we bring the right tires to you, mounted and balanced on the spot.

02

Wheel mounting

New wheels or a fresh set of tires on your existing rims — mounted correctly, torqued to spec, ready to drive on.

03

Computerized balancing

Vibration at highway speed usually means an unbalanced wheel. We fix it in the driveway with the same equipment a shop uses.

04

Flat repair

A nail or slow leak doesn't always mean a new tire. We patch what's fixable, right where your car is parked.

05

Trailer tires

Boat trailers, utility trailers, campers — trailer tires wear differently than car tires, and we stock for that.

OUR STORY

Nearly 20 years in tires before Chyne Tire existed

Owner Mark Deyne spent close to two decades working at his family's tire shop before setting out on his own. In 2020, that experience became Chyne Tire — built from the ground up as a mobile operation, bringing the same expertise directly to customers instead of asking them to come to a storefront.

Since opening, the team has served hundreds of customers across Volusia and Seminole County — every job done at the customer's home, office, or wherever the car happens to be parked.

OUR MISSION

Keeping cars road-ready, and families safe

Chyne Tire is built around one idea: reliable tire work at a fair price, without asking anyone to rearrange their day around it. Whether that's a driveway, a workplace parking lot, or somewhere in between, the standard doesn't change — the same tools and know-how a shop would use, brought directly to wherever the car is.
